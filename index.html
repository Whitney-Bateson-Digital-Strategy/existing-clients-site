Do I have to pick a design again?
No. You can keep the design you already chose. If you'd like a different one of the four, switching is no extra charge.
Do I have to redo the work I did before?
No. If your site is live, we start from it and you only send what you want changed. If it isn't, we work from the copy document you started in the Wellness Pro Website System.
I never finished my copy. Is that a problem?
Not at all. Nobody shows up with perfect copy. Send your drafts, however rough, and we'll edit them. If you haven't started, we can write it for you as an add-on.
How much does it cost?
It depends on where your site is now and where you want it to go. Moving a live site with a few updates is the simplest. Finishing a site that never launched, or having us write your copy, takes more. Tell us what you need and we'll send you a quote before anything starts.
How long does it take?
Most websites launch three to six weeks after kickoff. The biggest factor is how ready your copy and branding are. Once we have everything, it's two weeks to launch.
Do I need to get on a call?
No. Everything happens over email and on your project roadmap, so you can do it on your own schedule.
